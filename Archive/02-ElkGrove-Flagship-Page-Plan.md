# Elk Grove Roofing Flagship — Page Plan (Working Draft)

**Purpose:** The owned lead-gen asset. A deep, bounded local roofing authority site for Elk Grove that ranks for the searches homeowners actually make, wins AI answers, and routes exclusive calls to your founding-partner roofer. This is the case study that sells the productized offer (doc \#1) and the first real product on the directory-factory rails.

**Scope discipline (this is the point):** \~**40–70 excellent pages**, not thousands. Depth in one market, not breadth. This is what fixes the Sonora "content takes forever" problem — the surface area is small and the quality bar is reachable.

**Where your existing work plugs in:** your roofing **keyword research** → the keyword→page map in §3. Your existing **roofing articles** → seed the FAQ/guide layer in §4. Your **market research** → competitor targeting and which location pages are worth building (§5, §8).

---

## 1\. Strategy in one paragraph

Homeowners search two ways: **commercial** ("roof replacement cost Elk Grove," "emergency roof repair near me") and **informational** ("how long does a roof last," "signs I need a new roof"). The **money is in commercial \+ local intent** — those clicks turn into calls. Informational/FAQ content is top-of-funnel: it earns rankings, links, and AI citations, then internally links down to the money pages. We weight the build toward commercial/local and use FAQ as the magnet — **not** the other way around, because AI Overviews increasingly answer pure informational queries inline (the zero-click risk). Every page carries a clear call/quote CTA and is tied to the partner's Google Business Profile, which is the fastest local lead channel while organic ramps.

## 3\. Page-type templates

### Money / service page (`/roof-replacement`, `/roof-repair`, …)

- **Intent:** commercial. Homeowner ready or close to ready.  
- **Answer-first block up top:** 2–3 sentences answering the core question \+ a cost/what- to-expect snapshot (AEO-friendly, citable).  
- Sections: what's included → process/timeline → local specifics (Elk Grove climate, permits, HOA/tile prevalence) → materials options → cost range (link to cost page) → financing → why \[partner\] → reviews → FAQ (schema) → CTA.  
- **Schema:** `Service` \+ `LocalBusiness` (partner) \+ `FAQPage` \+ `BreadcrumbList`.  
- **Internal links:** to relevant materials, cost, financing, and the Elk Grove hub.

### Cost / pricing page (`/roof-replacement-cost`)

- **Highest commercial intent** — build this early and make it the best on the SERP.  
- Answer-first: honest local price bands by roof size/material (calibrate to partner's real numbers), what drives cost up/down, insurance angle, financing.  
- This is the page AI answer engines love to cite *and* that converts. Keep numbers real.

### Material page (`/roofing-materials/tile`, …)

- Comparison intent. Pros/cons, lifespan, cost, best-for, local relevance (tile common in CA), links to replacement \+ cost pages.

### Location / service-area page (`/service-area/elk-grove`, …)

- **Only build where the partner actually serves and demand exists** (avoid doorway-page thinness). Each must be genuinely local: neighborhoods served, local landmarks, climate, a local job example/photo, partner's proximity, area-specific FAQ.  
- Elk Grove is the flagship hub and gets the most depth; satellites are lighter but never empty.

### FAQ / AEO page (`/faq/...`)

- **Intent:** informational, top-of-funnel. One question per page (or grouped hub \+ anchors).  
- Structure: the question as H1 → **direct answer in the first 2–3 sentences** (this is what gets cited and what wins featured snippets/AI Overviews) → detail → local angle → internal link down to the relevant money page → soft CTA.  
- **Schema:** `FAQPage` / `QAPage`. Concise, factual, no fluff.  
- Purpose is rankings \+ links \+ AI citation that funnel to money pages — accept that some will be zero-click and judge them on the assists they create, not direct calls.

### Guide / blog (`/guides/...`)

- Long-form link magnets: "Elk Grove roofing in wildfire/heat season," "storm damage insurance claim walkthrough," "roof maintenance checklist." Seasonal, shareable, link to services.

## 4\. AEO layer (be the answer, then win the click)

- **Answer-first blocks** on every page — lead with the direct answer, then elaborate.  
- **FAQ schema** everywhere it fits.  
- **Entity alignment:** consistent NAP \+ partner brand across site, GBP, citations, so Google/LLMs treat them as one trusted entity.  
- **`llms.txt`** (reuse the factory builder) mapping the site for AI crawlers.  
- **Concise, citable, sourced** facts. AI engines cite specific, verifiable answers and skip hype — which conveniently is also good SEO and good UX.  
- Seed this layer from your **existing roofing articles** — rework them into answer-first format rather than writing from scratch.

---

## 5\. Conversion architecture (where leads actually come from)

- **Click-to-call** everywhere on mobile, with a **call-tracking number** so every call is attributed and routed to the partner.  
- **Quote form** → routed to partner (and to you for tracking) with instant notification; speed-to-lead is everything in roofing.  
- **Financing CTA** (roofing is a financed purchase for many) — strong conversion lever.  
- **Google Business Profile** front and center — reviews, photos, click-to-call. **GBP is the fastest lead channel and often outruns organic in the first 90 days**, so it's not an afterthought; it's phase one.  
- **Trust stack** on every money page: reviews, warranty, licensing, local proof photos.

---

## 6\. Local SEO / GBP plan (the fast channel)

1. Fully optimize the partner's **GBP** — categories, services, service areas, weekly posts, photo cadence, Q\&A seeded.  
2. **Review engine** — systematic review requests after every job (this moves GBP rank and trust faster than almost anything).  
3. **Citations / NAP consistency** across directories.  
4. **Local links** — suppliers, associations, sponsorships, local press.  
5. Tie GBP \+ site \+ citations into one consistent entity (see AEO §4).

## 7\. Measurement (borrow the doc's one genuinely good loop)

- **Lead tracking first:** calls (tracked number), form fills, quotes booked, jobs closed, revenue. This is the number that renews the retainer and proves the case study.  
- **Monthly GSC refresh loop:** pull 90 days; attack **striking-distance** queries (avg position 5–20) by strengthening those pages; build pages for queries you rank for but didn't target; content-gap vs. top local competitors; refresh decaying pages. This turns a static site into a compounding asset for a few hours a month.  
- Report to the partner in **calls and jobs**, never vanity rankings.

## 10\. Open items / decisions before build

1. **Confirm the founding partner** — their real service area, materials, price bands, and capacity feed the location pages, cost page, and lead routing. Build around a real roofer, not a hypothetical.  
2. **Domain** — new Elk Grove roofing domain vs. subfolder. (A focused local domain is usually cleaner for a rank-and-partner asset; decide with the ownership structure.)
