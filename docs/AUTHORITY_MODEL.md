# Local Authority Model

> Strategy, not code. This document explains *why* the framework is built the way it is.
> For *what* to build, see `FRAMEWORK_PRD.md`.

---

## Core principle

People do not search for websites. They search for answers.

The goal of this platform is to become the best answer for every important question
related to a trade within a defined geographic market.

**Authority comes before lead generation. Lead generation follows authority.**

---

## The Authority Pyramid

```
                    Trust
             Local Expertise
           Helpful Knowledge
         Problems & Solutions
              Service Pages
             Business Entity
```

Each layer reinforces the one above it. A site that skips to the top — trust signals
without knowledge, reviews without expertise — has nothing holding it up.

---

## The authority formula

```
Business + Local Knowledge + Educational Content
        + Technical Excellence + Structured Data + Consistency
        = Authority

Authority + Trust + Visibility = Leads
```

---

## The Local Knowledge Layer

**This is the competitive advantage.**

A generic language model knows how roofs and water heaters work. It does not know:

- Local weather and seasonal patterns
- Elevation and its effects
- Water quality and hardness
- Soil conditions
- Building history and construction eras
- Neighborhood characteristics
- Local regulations and permit process
- Common local failure modes

Every authority website must contain information that a generic model cannot reproduce
and a competitor cannot copy from Wikipedia.

Examples of the layer in practice:

- Why homes above 4,500 feet in Tuolumne County see different heating loads
- Which neighborhoods still have galvanized plumbing
- How hard the local groundwater actually is
- What freezing conditions look like on a specific ridge
- Which seasonal issues local homeowners actually encounter

That is defensible. Generic content is not.

## Knowledge structure

Every service expands into a cluster, not a landing page:

```
Service
  → Problems
  → Solutions
  → Maintenance
  → Costs
  → Frequently Asked Questions
  → Local Considerations
  → Related Services
  → Emergency Situations
  → Educational Articles
```

This creates topical authority rather than isolated pages.

### The mindset shift

We are not trying to rank pages. We are trying to answer every important question a
homeowner could reasonably have.

**Old SEO mindset:**

```
Water Heater Repair Sonora
```

**Authority mindset:**

```
Water Heater Repair
  → Why did it fail?
  → Should I repair or replace?
  → Expected lifespan
  → Hard water effects
  → Local water conditions
  → Maintenance schedule
  → Tank vs. tankless
  → Emergency shutdown
```

The second approach owns the topic.

## Geographic strategy

Large cities become hub websites. Smaller surrounding communities become location pages
within the nearest hub.

```
Roof Repair Modesto
  → Riverbank
  → Oakdale
  → Salida
  → Ripon
  → Ceres
  → Turlock
  → Manteca
```

When a surrounding market becomes large enough, it graduates to an independent hub with
its own domain and its own surrounding communities.

Market expansion is driven by opportunity — search demand, competition, population,
distance from existing hubs, commercial value — not by buying every available
exact-match domain.

## Human expertise layer

AI accelerates content production. **Humans create authority.**

Authority comes from information AI cannot invent:

- Local climate and conditions
- Local construction practices
- Local history
- Technician experience
- Community knowledge
- Actual completed projects
- Real customer questions

This layer is the only thing separating an authority website from an AI-generated
website.

### Where AI fits

Content generation is execution, not strategy:

```
Knowledge Map → Content Strategy → AI drafting → Human Expertise → Authority Website
```

The strategy comes first. Tools help execute it.

---

## The prediction this rests on

There will be two kinds of local business websites:

**Type A — SEO factories.** Thirty AI articles, zero local knowledge. They all sound
alike.

**Type B — Authority websites.** Human expertise, AI-assisted writing, local knowledge,
structured data, helpful content, trust.

Google, AI assistants, and users will increasingly favor the second. The framework exists
to make Type B cheap to produce.

---

## Framework mission

Every website the framework generates should become:

- The most trusted resource in its market
- The most complete resource
- The most technically sound resource
- The most locally relevant resource

Lead generation is the natural consequence of becoming the local authority. It is not the
primary objective.

---

## The test

> "If I lived in this community and needed this service, would this be the most helpful
> website available?"

Every architectural and content decision should move a site closer to yes.
